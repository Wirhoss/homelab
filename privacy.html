Privacy Policy

Bushido Homelab · Last updated October 8, 2026

Who uses these apps

Bushido Homelab apps are used only by their owner. They are not offered to other people.

What data is accessed

When the owner authorizes a service (for example Google Drive), the apps access only the data needed for the owner's own automations, with the narrowest permission available. For Google Drive this is the drive.file scope: only files and folders the app itself creates.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed on infrastructure controlled by the owner to run personal automations. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets on that infrastructure. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the provider's account settings, e.g. myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Resumen en español
